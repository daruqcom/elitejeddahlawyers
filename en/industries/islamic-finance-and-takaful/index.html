A typical scenario

A customer defaults on murabaha financing and disagrees with the institution over how the late amount is calculated and whether a penalty may be imposed, a question that combines the contract with a Sharia rule.

Who supervises this sector?

Bodies connected with the activity here include the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA), the Capital Market Authority, the Insurance Authority and institutions' Sharia boards. Following their requirements is a core part of any ongoing legal support.

Jeddah and this sector

Most banking and financing institutions in Jeddah rely on Islamic products, so their disputes usually touch Sharia-compliant financing structures.

What we take on for your business

  • Reviewing your current contracts to find gaps before they become disputes
  • Checking your licences are complete and renewed on time
  • Defending your rights before the competent body when a dispute arises
  • Advice that matches the stage your business has reached

Clauses we do not leave to the other side's interpretation

  • A precise scope of work and timetable
  • The liability cap, penalties and the conditions for applying them
  • The governing law and the forum for disputes
  • Termination terms, handover of documents and amounts owed

What sector knowledge is worth

A lawyer who does not know how your business runs can give advice that is sound in theory and miss the operating detail that changes the outcome. The better a lawyer understands the sector, the less you have to explain and the more precise the advice becomes.

Prevention costs less than a fix

Most disputes the lawyers see could have been avoided with a simple review of the contract or licence early on. An annual review of your legal file costs far less than dealing with a violation after it happens.

Papers that speed up the assessment

Attach what you have: a commercial licence, contracts in force, and correspondence with the other party or the regulator, along with a note on the size of your business. The clearer the file, the more precise the opinion.
